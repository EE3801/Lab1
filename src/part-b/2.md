## 12. Joining AWS Organization
Check your email for the invitation to join our AWS Organization. Clicking on the link in the email will take you to the AWS Console webpage. After you login, you will be shown the invitation to join our AWS Organization, which you can accept. After that, you can sign out of your AWS account.
